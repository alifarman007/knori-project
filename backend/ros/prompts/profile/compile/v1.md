Write the short profile KnoRI keeps of {{ user_name }}.

It has two readers, and both matter:

1. **{{ user_name }} themselves.** It is shown to them on their memory page. They must be
   able to read every line and recognise it as a fair account of what they have said.
2. **KnoRI**, before every reply. It is the difference between a chat that remembers and
   one that starts from nothing each time.

So: accurate, plain, and nothing in it they did not put there. Nothing flattering, nothing
clinical, nothing they would be surprised to see written down about them.

## What to produce

`sections`, with exactly these five headings in this order, and `profile_text`, which is
the same content rendered as markdown (`## ` before each heading, one `-` bullet per line,
nothing else). Under 900 tokens in total; shorter is better.

**About {{ user_name }}** — the steady facts of their life from what they told KnoRI: work,
where they are, who is around them, how their days are shaped. Six lines at most.

**What matters to them** — what they said they care about, want, or are working towards.
Their goals and the things they protect. Five lines at most.

**What KnoRI has recognized** — one line per accepted Recognition, in plain words. If there
are none, one line saying so. Never add a claim that is not in the accepted list.

**What they corrected** — what they have told KnoRI is wrong or only partly right, in their
framing. These outrank everything else in this profile. If there are none, one line saying
so.

**How to talk with them** — their preferred style, anything they asked KnoRI to leave
alone, and any plainly stated preference about how to be spoken to. Four lines at most.

## Rules

1. Every line traces back to something in the input above. If it is not there, it does not
   go in. No filling gaps, no "seems to", no joining two facts into a third.
2. Third person, present tense, one fact per line, no sub-clauses. Write "Works as a
   nurse; two night shifts a week." not a paragraph.
3. Where the input disagrees with itself, the corrections win, then the newest memory.
4. No labels or personality words: no "perfectionist", "introvert", "driven", "anxious".
   Describe what they do and say, not what they are.
5. Nothing about anyone else as a fact. "Has a sister she speaks to most weeks" describes
   {{ user_name }}. "Her sister is difficult" does not belong here at all.
6. Sensitive things they told KnoRI outright may be recorded here plainly — they said it,
   and they can see and delete it. Never build on one, never explain anything by one.
7. Anything under `<off_limits>` is named in the last section only, as a subject to avoid.
   It appears nowhere else.
8. This is a record, not a portrait. If there is little input, write a short profile. Four
   honest lines beat twenty invented ones.
